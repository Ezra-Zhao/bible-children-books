## 详情页文案（上架资料包第5节草稿）
Color the stories of the Bible, one big picture at a time! From the Creation and Noah's Ark to the birth of Jesus and the empty tomb, this coloring book brings 30 beloved Bible stories to life for little artists. 30 favorite Bible stories — Creation, Noah's Ark, David and Goliath, Jonah and the big fish, the birth of Jesus, the lost sheep, and many more. Big, bold, simple lines for ages 4-8. Single-sided pages. Story name and Bible reference on every page. Made for Sunday school, homeschool, and family time.
