## 定价测算（Amazon.com，美国站）
印刷成本公式：Premium Color、24–40 页为**固定价**——常规开本 $3.60/本，大开本（Large Trim）$4.20/本。8.5×8.5 属大开本口径的可能性高，**按下表以 $4.20 保守口径为主算**，$3.60 并列参考。
版税公式（定价 ≥ $9.99）：版税 = 定价 × 60% − 印刷成本（定价 < $9.99 的纸书版税率只有 50%，不要定到 9.99 以下）。

| 定价 | 版税（成本 $4.20 保守口径） | 版税（成本 $3.60 参考口径） |
|---|---|---|
| $9.99 | $9.99×0.6 − $4.20 = **$1.79 / 本** | $2.39 / 本 |
| $11.99 | $11.99×0.6 − $4.20 = **$2.99 / 本** | $3.59 / 本 |

- 保本最低定价：$4.20 ÷ 0.6 = $7.00（低于此价倒贴）。
- **建议定价：$11.99**。理由：美亚同类圣经故事绘本（8.5×8.5 平装）主流带 $9.99–$12.99；$11.99 留出促销降价空间（降到 $9.99 仍有 $1.79），且单本版税接近 $3。首发也可 $9.99 求评论量、稳定后调 $11.99——二选一在上传时定。
- 估算口径说明：以上为印刷成本与版税的静态测算，不含广告与退货；费率以 KDP 官方成本表为准，KDP 会不定期调价。

## 详情页文案（Description 草稿）
**Two by two, the animals came!**

Climb aboard the ark with Noah in this gentle retelling of one of the Bible's most loved stories — made just for little hearts.

From the first tap of Noah's hammer to the rainbow in the sky, every page pairs a short, read-aloud sentence with soft watercolor art full of friendly animals: stomping elephants, tall giraffes, hopping rabbits, and a panda who loves his bamboo.

- One simple sentence per page — perfect for ages 4–8 and early readers
- Cozy bedtime pacing with a warm, promise-filled ending: *God always keeps His promises*
- Big, friendly watercolor illustrations on every page
- A sweet gift for Sunday school, baptisms, birthdays, and baby showers

Based on Genesis chapters 6–9. Retold and illustrated with love by Guangyi Zhao.
